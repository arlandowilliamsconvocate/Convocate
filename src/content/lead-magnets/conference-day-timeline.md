# Conference Day Timeline

A run-of-show for a 100 to 500 seat conference: what has to be done a month out, a week out and the day before, an hour-by-hour sheet for the day itself, and who owns each line.

The single biggest difference between a calm conference and a frantic one is not experience. It is that every line on the day has a name next to it. This template gives each line an owner. Fill in the real names before the week of the event, and make sure each person has a printed copy of the part they own.

---

## Who owns what

Keep the roles small and specific. One person can hold two roles at a 100 seat event; at 400 they should not.

| Role | Owns | In our event |
|---|---|---|
| **Event lead** | The whole day, the decisions, the walk-through, the final call on anything unplanned | |
| **Program lead** | The stage: session start and end times, the speaker, transitions, the timer | |
| **Registration lead** | The front desk, badges, the attendee list, walk-ins, questions at the door | |
| **AV lead** | Sound, microphones, screens, slides, recording, the technician relationship | |
| **Speaker host** | The speaker's arrival, green room, water, timings, transport, thank-you | |
| **Food lead** | Catering arrival, set-up, service times, dietary meals, coffee, cleanup | |
| **Volunteer coordinator** | Who is where and when, briefings, breaks, food for volunteers | |
| **Finance lead** | The cash float, card reader, receipts, on-site payments, the deposit return | |
| **Facilities liaison** | The venue's own staff, doors, temperature, access, power, problems | |

Write the cell phone number for each role next to the name on your printed copy. Not the email address.

## A month out

The month before is where the day is won or lost. Everything here should be finished before the last week, so the last week is only confirmation.

**Contracts and money**
- [ ] Every contract signed and every signed copy saved in one place. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Payment schedule checked against the calendar, with the next due date on it. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Venue deposit paid, and the receipt saved. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Speaker agreement signed, including travel, lodging and recording terms. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Insurance certificate issued and sent to the venue, in the wording they asked for. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Any permit or license confirmed as approved, not just applied for. Owner: event lead

**The room and the stage**
- [ ] Final floor plan agreed with the venue, with the layout drawn and the seat count checked. Owner: event lead
- [ ] AV plan written: what the venue provides, what you bring, what you rent, and who operates it. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] Speaker's technical requirements collected and sent to the AV lead and the venue. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] Load-in and load-out times confirmed in writing, including the day before. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] Power, internet and rigging confirmed. Owner: AV lead

**The program**
- [ ] The final schedule written down with real times, including breaks and transitions. Owner: program lead
- [ ] Every session has a named speaker, a title and a length. Owner: program lead
- [ ] Each speaker told their start time, session length, room, and what equipment will be there. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] A plan for the gap between sessions: how long, who speaks, what is on screen. Owner: program lead

**People**
- [ ] Registration open, and the confirmation email checked by sending one to yourself. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Roles assigned by name, with a phone list. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Volunteer recruitment finished, and a briefing session scheduled. Owner: volunteer coordinator
- [ ] Catering final numbers policy understood: the deadline, and the cost of raising it. Owner: food lead
- [ ] Dietary requirements collected from registrations and sent to the caterer. Owner: food lead

**Materials**
- [ ] Badges designed and ordered, with spares. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Signs designed and ordered: entrance, registration, rooms, sessions, restrooms, food. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Printed programs or handouts at the printer, with a proof approved. Owner: program lead
- [ ] Any gift, book or handout for attendees confirmed as delivered or arriving in time. Owner: event lead

---

## Two weeks out

- [ ] Confirmation email sent to every registrant with the date, the address, parking, the start time and what to bring. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Speaker travel booked and confirmations sent to the speaker. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] Hotel bookings confirmed for the speaker, for the right nights. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] Final catering numbers given, or the deadline put in the calendar with a reminder. Owner: food lead
- [ ] AV equipment list confirmed with the supplier, including delivery times. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] Volunteer briefing held: who does what, where to stand, who to call. Owner: volunteer coordinator
- [ ] A walk-through booked with the venue, with the person who will actually be there on the day. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Registration list printed as a backup, in case the internet or the app fails. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] A "who does what if" list written: who calls the venue, who calls the caterer, who talks to attendees. Owner: event lead

---

## A week out

- [ ] Every vendor reconfirmed by phone or email: arrival time, contact name, what they are bringing. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Speaker reconfirmed: flights, arrival time, hotel, session time, who meets them. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] Room set-up confirmed against the plan: seats, stage, tables, AV position, registration table. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] Signs checked for spelling, and for the right room names. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Badges counted, sorted and packed alphabetically. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Registration desk kit packed: tape, scissors, pens, stapler, spare badge holders, chargers, a clipboard, a printed list. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Cash float arranged if you are taking money on site, and the card reader tested. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Volunteer roster final, with arrival times and breaks. Owner: volunteer coordinator
- [ ] Volunteer food and water arranged — they will not get to the buffet. Owner: volunteer coordinator
- [ ] Final headcount sent to the caterer, with any dietary updates. Owner: food lead
- [ ] Parking, directions and accessibility details sent to attendees one more time. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Every owner has a printed copy of the day sheet, and knows their arrival time. Owner: event lead

---

## The day before

This is a working day, not a day off. Most of what goes wrong on the morning could have been caught here.

- [ ] **Set-up done and the room walked.** Seats in place, stage set, tables where the plan says. Owner: event lead
- [ ] **AV tested with real content**: a slide deck from the speaker's laptop, a video with sound, both microphones, the clicker. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] **Every microphone tested from the back of the room**, not from the stage. Owner: AV lead
- [ ] **Speaker arrives, walks the room, checks the stage, meets the AV lead.** Owner: speaker host
- [ ] **Registration desk set up** with badges, lists and the kit, so the morning is only opening boxes. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] **Signs placed or staged** where they will go, with the tape and stands ready. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] **Catering arrival time confirmed** with the caterer, and the loading door arranged. Owner: food lead
- [ ] **Water for the speaker and the stage** delivered and placed. Owner: speaker host
- [ ] **Doors, lights, temperature and restrooms checked.** Owner: facilities liaison
- [ ] **A full run of the schedule read aloud** with the program lead and the AV lead, session by session. Owner: event lead
- [ ] **Phone numbers checked**: every owner's number works, and the venue contact answers. Owner: event lead
- [ ] **Volunteers briefed on site**, walking the routes they will walk tomorrow. Owner: volunteer coordinator
- [ ] **Everyone sent home at a reasonable hour.** A tired team makes the morning harder than any missing sign. Owner: event lead

## The day of: hour by hour

Clock times below are an example for a 9 a.m. start. Write your own in the column on your printed copy. The order matters more than the exact times.

| Time | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **5:30** | Event lead and AV lead arrive. Unlock, lights on, walk the room, check for anything changed overnight. | Event lead |
| **6:00** | AV powered up and tested again. Microphones, playback, slides, confidence monitor, recording if any. | AV lead |
| **6:15** | Catering arrives; food lead takes delivery and confirms service times with the caterer. | Food lead |
| **6:30** | Registration desk opened up: badges out, lists out, kit out, signs up at the entrance. | Registration lead |
| **6:45** | Volunteers arrive, sign in, get a badge and a shirt, get their position and their break. | Volunteer coordinator |
| **7:00** | Speaker arrives at the venue, or is collected. Green room ready, water, quiet, a clock. | Speaker host |
| **7:15** | Coffee and arrival food ready. Doors open to the lobby. Early attendees are always early. | Food lead |
| **7:30** | Registration opens. Two lines if you can: pre-registered, and questions or walk-ins. | Registration lead |
| **8:00** | Speaker's technical check on stage with the AV lead. Slides, microphone, clicker, lighting. | AV lead |
| **8:15** | Event lead and program lead walk the room together. Any seat blocking a view gets moved now. | Event lead |
| **8:30** | Final call for the opening session. Volunteers guide people in and close the back doors. | Volunteer coordinator |
| **8:45** | Speaker waits in the wings. Phone on silent. Timer set. Opening slide up. | Speaker host |
| **9:00** | **Opening session starts.** Program lead keeps the clock; AV lead keeps the sound. | Program lead |
| **9:05** | Registration desk records late arrivals quietly, outside the doors, without blocking the entrance. | Registration lead |
| **9:45** | Session ends. Transition announced with the next start time, not just "we'll begin shortly". | Program lead |
| **10:00** | Break. Coffee refilled, water stations checked, dietary food out and labeled. | Food lead |
| **10:15** | Next session starts on time even if the room is not full. Starting late punishes the people who came on time. | Program lead |
| **11:00** | Mid-morning check with every owner: what is broken, what is missing, what needs a decision. | Event lead |
| **11:45** | Lunch set-up finished before the session ends, not during the rush. | Food lead |
| **12:00** | Lunch. Dietary meals plated or labeled first, and staff told where they are. | Food lead |
| **12:15** | Volunteers take their breaks in shifts, and eat. Someone must cover the desk. | Volunteer coordinator |
| **12:45** | Speaker host checks the afternoon speaker is ready, fed, watered, and has seen the room. | Speaker host |
| **1:00** | Afternoon session starts. Announce the time clearly so people come back. | Program lead |
| **2:00** | Registration desk reconciles: who came, who did not, who paid on site. | Registration lead |
| **2:30** | Second break. Coffee is lighter now; do not over-order and do not run out of water. | Food lead |
| **3:00** | Final session. Program lead confirms the closing remarks and who gives them. | Program lead |
| **3:45** | Closing session. Thank the speaker, the volunteers, the venue. Announce where to find the recording or materials. | Event lead |
| **4:00** | **Event ends.** Volunteers guide people out; registration desk stays open for questions and lost property. | Registration lead |
| **4:15** | Speaker's transport arranged, gift or honorarium handed over, thank-you said in person. | Speaker host |
| **4:30** | Venue walk-through with the facilities liaison: damage, leftover food, missing equipment, the deposit. | Event lead |
| **5:00** | Load-out begins, inside the contracted window. Empty cases, borrowed gear, signs down. | AV lead |
| **6:00** | Everything out. Final sweep of every room, including the green room and the restrooms, for belongings. | Event lead |
| **6:30** | Quick team debrief while it is fresh: three things that worked, three that did not, written down. | Event lead |

## When it goes wrong: five decisions to make in advance

Every conference has at least one of these. Decide the answer before the day, so nobody is deciding it in a corridor.

1. **The speaker is late.** Who fills the time, for how long, and what is on screen? Have a five minute and a fifteen minute option, and have someone briefed to deliver them.
2. **The microphone fails.** Where is the spare, who fetches it, and who tells the room? Never let a technician troubleshoot in silence while the room watches.
3. **More people arrive than seats.** Which room or space opens up, who decides, and who moves the chairs? Decide the answer with the venue the day before.
4. **Fewer people arrive than expected.** Which seats come out, which food gets held back, and who tells the caterer — without making the room feel empty.
5. **Something is unsafe.** Who has the authority to stop a session, clear a room or call emergency services, and does that person know it is them? Write the name down.

## The first 72 hours after

- [ ] Thank-you note to the speaker, the venue, the caterer, the volunteers. Same week, not next month. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Final invoices checked against the contract, including service charges and overtime. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Damage deposit chased. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Payments and refunds reconciled against the attendee list. Owner: finance lead
- [ ] Recording, slides and materials sent to attendees, if you promised them. Owner: program lead
- [ ] Attendee feedback collected with a short form, not a long survey. Owner: registration lead
- [ ] Numbers written down while they are fresh: registered, attended, no-shows, income, final spend. Owner: event lead
- [ ] Debrief notes filed with next year's date. Owner: event lead
